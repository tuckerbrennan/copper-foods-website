THE COPPER CANYON EXPERIENCE

Good food.
Great drinks.
Better together.

Inspired by the creative spirit of Southwestern cooking, chef and owner Michael Krikorian brought something different to Atlantic Highlands: adventurous flavors, an inviting room, and plenty of reasons to stay for another round.

From Southwestern crab cakes and steaks to the tequila bar and its signature margaritas, Copper Canyon is a local favorite for everyday lunches, long dinners, and special occasions.
