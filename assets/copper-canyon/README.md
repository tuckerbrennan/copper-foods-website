# Copper Canyon — archived website imagery

These are **real image files** downloaded from the existing, public Copper Canyon website and its Wix media CDN, archived in the user's personal Google Drive, then committed to this repository for the presentation.

**Asset origin:** https://www.thecoppercanyon.com/ and https://www.thecoppercanyon.com/gallery  
**Drive archive (32 images):** https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ZypQCdOp-fW3tI3ZIGz4fC2wF9hfEyyq  
**Restaurant-owned website images, not stock illustrations:** Images were selected by inspecting actual visual contents.

## Presentation constraints

- **Do not add unrelated stock photographs** to any page.
- **Do not display Copper Canyon photos as Higo or Gaslight photos**. On the parent site, Higo and Gaslight use text-based branded visual panels until approved photography is available.
- The Instagram `@thecoppercanyon` public crawler could not access post images without authentication. **No Instagram images were used.**
- External image hotlinks to Wix and Unsplash were removed from the currently published HTML pages.
- Menus/prices remain based on the user's uploaded lunch and dinner photographs; no new prices or food claims were inferred from photos.
- The restaurant should confirm approval/ownership of all original website photography before production deployment.

For additional available photos, see the 32-image Drive archive and the public-site crawl manifest.
